Return integrations connect your warehouse management system (WMS), third-party logistics provider (3PL), or fulfillment platform directly to Redo. When a customer requests a return through Redo, we automatically create a corresponding return merchandise authorization (RMA) in your external system—eliminating manual data entry, reducing processing errors, and ensuring your warehouse team has complete visibility into incoming returns.

When you connect your WMS or 3PL to Redo, the entire return flow happens automatically. Once a return is approved in Redo, an RMA is created in your warehouse system with all relevant details. After your warehouse receives and processes the return, Redo automatically triggers the refund or sends out the exchange item to the customer—no manual intervention needed.
