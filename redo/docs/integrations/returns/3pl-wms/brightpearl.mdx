## What is Brightpearl?

Brightpearl is a retail operations platform and ERP system designed for multi-channel retailers and wholesalers. It provides comprehensive order management, inventory control, warehouse management, purchasing, and financial management capabilities in a single platform.

**Primary Purpose:** Brightpearl delivers complete retail operations management for growing brands, handling everything from order processing and inventory tracking to accounting and reporting. Built for omnichannel retail, Brightpearl helps businesses scale their operations across multiple sales channels while maintaining accurate financial records.

## What Does the Integration Do?

When a customer initiates a return through Redo, the system automatically creates a Sales Credit in Brightpearl linked to the original sales order. This ensures accurate financial tracking, proper inventory management, and seamless integration with your accounting workflows.

Products are mapped using SKUs, and returns are linked to the original Brightpearl sales order using the Shopify order ID. The integration validates that products exist on the original sales order, retrieves pricing and tax information from the original transaction, and creates a properly formatted sales credit with accurate amounts. Return values are calculated based on the original product prices, taxes, and any adjustments, ensuring financial accuracy.

The sales credit references the parent sales order in Brightpearl, maintaining complete audit trails and enabling proper accounting reconciliation. Each product line in the return is matched to the corresponding line in the original sales order, preserving product IDs, tax codes, and pricing details.

Optionally, Redo can also process the return automatically once the sales credit reaches a Brightpearl status you choose.

## How to Set It Up

Connecting Brightpearl uses OAuth. You authorize Redo from inside Brightpearl, so there are no API keys or tokens to copy anywhere.

<Warning>
  **Brightpearl must approve your account before you can connect.** The Redo
  Returns app is currently in Brightpearl's early access program, which means
  Brightpearl has to individually approve each account that installs it. This
  approval is requested by Redo, not by you, and it is the most common reason a
  connection attempt fails. Start this before you plan to connect. See
  [Prerequisites](#prerequisites).
</Warning>

### Prerequisites

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Brightpearl approval for the Redo Returns app" icon="circle-check">
  Send your Brightpearl **account code** to your Redo representative. Redo adds
  it to the Redo Returns app's early access list, and a Brightpearl
  administrator then verifies it.

  Your account code is the short, all-lowercase identifier for your Brightpearl
  account, for example `examplebrand`. It is not your company's display name.

  Until Brightpearl completes this approval, the connection will fail. You
  cannot request the approval yourself, because the app allowlist belongs to
  Redo's Brightpearl developer account.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Active Brightpearl account with API access" icon="circle-check">
  You need an active Brightpearl subscription with API access enabled.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A Brightpearl user who can authorize apps" icon="key">
  The person completing the connection needs a Brightpearl login with
  permission to authorize a third-party app.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Administrative access to Redo" icon="key">
  You need administrative access to your Redo merchant dashboard to configure
  integrations.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Orders synced to Brightpearl" icon="box">
  Your sales orders must already be synced to Brightpearl (typically through
  your e-commerce platform integration like Shopify). Returns cannot be created
  for orders that don't exist as sales orders in Brightpearl.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Shopify order ID field configuration" icon="database">
  Know which field in your Brightpearl sales orders contains the Shopify order
  ID. Common options include:
  - `customerRef` (default)
  - `reference`
  - Custom field

  This field must be populated with `SO{shopify_order_id}` format (e.g.,
  `SO1234567890`).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Product SKUs aligned" icon="barcode">
  Ensure product SKUs in your Brightpearl sales orders match the SKUs in Redo.
  The integration uses SKUs to match returned products to sales order line
  items.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Configuration Steps

<Steps>
<Step title="Confirm your account is approved">
  Check with your Redo representative that Brightpearl has verified your
  account code for the Redo Returns app. If you connect before this is done,
  the authorization will fail at the last step.
</Step>

<Step title="Open the Brightpearl integration">
  In the Redo merchant dashboard, go to **Settings** > **Integrations** and
  open the **Brightpearl** integration.
</Step>

<Step title="Click Connect">
  On the **Returns** row, click **Connect**.
</Step>

<Step title="Enter your Brightpearl account code">
  <ParamField path="account" type="string" required>
    Your Brightpearl account code, all lowercase, for example `examplebrand`.
    This is the same code you gave your Redo representative for approval, and
    it is the only value you need to enter.
  </ParamField>

  Then click **Connect via OAuth**.
</Step>

<Step title="Authorize Redo in Brightpearl">
  You are redirected to Brightpearl. Sign in if prompted and approve the Redo
  Returns app. Brightpearl then redirects you back to Redo.

  Redo stores the connection automatically. There is no token or data center
  URL to copy back.
</Step>

<Step title="Verify the connection">
  Back in Redo, the Brightpearl integration shows as connected and the
  **Returns** row shows as active. Returns sync is on as soon as the
  connection succeeds, with no additional toggle to enable.
</Step>

<Step title="Test the integration">
  Create a test return to verify the integration is working correctly:

  1. Initiate a test return through your Redo return portal
  2. Check the return timeline in Redo for a Brightpearl sales credit creation entry
  3. Log in to Brightpearl to verify the sales credit was created
  4. Confirm the sales credit:
     - References the correct parent sales order
     - Contains accurate products, quantities, and SKUs
     - Has correct pricing and tax calculations
     - Shows proper linkage in Brightpearl's order hierarchy
</Step>
</Steps>

### Optional Settings

After connecting, the **Manage** view on the Returns row exposes settings that
are specific to your Brightpearl account. All of them are optional, and the
integration creates sales credits without them.

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Sales credit custom fields" icon="tag">
  Write Redo values onto custom fields on the sales credit, for example putting
  the return tracking number on a custom field in your Brightpearl account.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Payment settings" icon="credit-card">
  Optionally mark the sales credit as paid automatically, and map each Redo
  compensation method (refund, exchange, store credit) to a Brightpearl payment
  method code.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Sales credit status IDs" icon="list-check">
  Drive Redo's behavior from Brightpearl sales credit statuses:
  - **Processable status IDs** trigger automatic processing of the return in Redo.
  - **Flaggable status IDs** leave the return open with a timeline note for manual review.

  Status IDs are specific to your Brightpearl account, so you need to supply
  the numeric IDs from your own status configuration.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## How Long Does It Take?

The connection itself takes a few minutes. The Brightpearl approval in front of
it is the long pole, and it is the step worth starting early.

| Step | Time | Description |
|------|------|-------------|
| Brightpearl account approval | Hours to a few days | Redo submits your account code; a Brightpearl administrator verifies it |
| Initial connection | 2-5 minutes | Enter your account code and authorize Redo in Brightpearl |
| Optional settings | 5-15 minutes | Custom fields, payment method codes, and status IDs, if you want them |
| First return test | 5-10 minutes | Create a test return and verify the sales credit in Brightpearl |

### Operational Timing

<Steps>
<Step title="Sales Credit Creation">
**Real-time (1-3 seconds)** after return is created.
</Step>

<Step title="Sales Order Lookup">
**Immediate** - Redo searches for the original sales order in Brightpearl using the Shopify order ID to ensure it exists before creating the sales credit.
</Step>

<Step title="Product Validation">
**Automatic** - Each returned product is validated against the original sales order to ensure SKUs match and proper pricing/tax codes are retrieved.
</Step>

<Step title="Financial Recording">
Sales credits are immediately visible in Brightpearl's sales order list and financial reports, enabling accurate accounting and reconciliation.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Important Considerations
<Warning>
**Product Matching Required:** All returned products must exist as line items on the original Brightpearl sales order with matching SKUs. If a product cannot be matched, the sales credit creation will fail.
</Warning>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Connection fails with 'redo-returns is not a recognised provider code'">
Brightpearl has not yet approved your account for the Redo Returns app. This is
the error you get when you complete the authorization in Brightpearl but your
account is not on the approved list.

**Solution:**
- Contact your Redo representative with your Brightpearl account code so they can submit it for approval
- Once Brightpearl confirms the approval, retry the connection from the start
- Asking your own Brightpearl account manager to expedite the approval is the fastest way to move it along, but Redo has to submit the request first

Nothing needs to change in your Redo settings, and re-entering the account code
will not resolve it until the approval lands.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Sales Credit Creation Failed: Original Sales Order Not Found">
This error occurs when Redo cannot locate the original sales order in Brightpearl.

**Solution:**
- Verify the order exists in Brightpearl as a sales order
- Ensure the field in Brightpearl contains the Shopify order ID in `SO{order_id}` format
- Confirm orders are syncing correctly from Shopify to Brightpearl
- Check if there's a delay in order sync between Shopify and Brightpearl
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Sales Credit Creation Failed: Sales Order Row Not Found">
This error means a product being returned doesn't exist as a line item on the original Brightpearl sales order, or the SKU doesn't match.

**Solution:**
- Verify the product SKU in Redo matches the SKU in the Brightpearl sales order exactly
- Check if the product was on the original order
- Review SKU mapping between your e-commerce platform and Brightpearl
- Ensure no SKU changes occurred after the order was placed
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Sales Credit Already Exists">
This message indicates a sales credit has already been created for this return.

**Solution:**
- Verify that the sales credit already exists for the corresponding sales order in Brightpearl
- If the sales credit is incorrect, delete it in Brightpearl and retry the sync from Redo
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Returns are not processing automatically">
Redo only processes returns automatically when the sales credit reaches a status
you have configured as processable.

**Solution:**
- Open **Manage** on the Returns row and confirm your processable status IDs are set
- Confirm the numeric status IDs match your own Brightpearl status configuration
- Remember that status IDs differ between Brightpearl accounts, so IDs from another merchant will not work
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
