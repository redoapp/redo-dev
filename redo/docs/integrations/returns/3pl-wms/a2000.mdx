## What is A2000?

A2000 is a warehouse management system (WMS) developed by GCS that provides
inventory management, order processing, and fulfillment capabilities for
e-commerce businesses.

## What Does the Integration Do?

A2000 has built a native integration with Redo directly into the A2000 package.
This integration is managed within A2000 itself.

The integration connects Redo and A2000 through two key touchpoints:

**RMA Import** When a customer initiates a return through Redo, the RMA (Return
Merchandise Authorization) is automatically imported into A2000, notifying the
warehouse that items are being returned.

**RMA Receipt Export** When returned items are received and processed in your
A2000 warehouse, A2000 exports the receipt confirmation back to Redo, which
automatically triggers the appropriate return outcome—whether that's a refund,
exchange, or store credit.

This eliminates manual data entry between systems and keeps your warehouse team
and customer service team in sync.

## How to Set It Up

The Redo integration is built into the A2000 package and is available to
customers on the current release. To enable the integration, contact A2000
support at [A2000support@gcsnet.com](mailto:A2000support@gcsnet.com).

## Support

For issues with the Redo integration configuration, return processing, or data
synchronization, contact **support@getredo.com**.

For issues with A2000 access or warehouse operations, contact your A2000 account
manager or support team.
