Redo can act as an [OpenID
Connect](https://openid.net/specs/openid-connect-core-1_0.html) relying party,
so your team signs in to the Merchant App with your identity provider (IdP)
instead of a Redo password or email code. Any conformant provider works,
including Okta, Microsoft Entra ID, Google Workspace, Auth0, and OneLogin.

<Info>
  This guide covers signing in to the Redo Merchant App at
  [app.getredo.com](https://app.getredo.com). It doesn't affect how shoppers
  sign in to your returns portal.
</Info>

## Overview

Redo uses the [authorization code
flow](https://openid.net/specs/openid-connect-core-1_0.html#CodeFlowAuth) with
a client secret, and discovers your provider's endpoints with [OpenID Connect
Discovery](https://openid.net/specs/openid-connect-discovery-1_0.html). When a
team member enters their email on the sign-in page and the domain is
configured for SSO, Redo redirects them to your IdP, exchanges the returned
code for an ID token, and matches its `email` claim to a user on your team.
Roles and permissions stay in Redo. Request parameters, token validation, and
error codes are in the [OpenID Connect reference](/docs/identity/oidc).


## Prerequisites

- The **Admin** role in Redo
- Admin access to your IdP
- Each team member provisioned with [SCIM](/docs/identity/scim-provisioning)
  or added under **Settings** → **General** → **Users**, with the email
  address your IdP will assert. SSO doesn't create users.

## Register Redo in your identity provider

Create a confidential OIDC client using the [client registration
values](/docs/identity/oidc#client-registration), assign the users or
groups who should have access, and note the **issuer URL**, **client ID**, and
**client secret**.

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Okta">
  Follow [Create OIDC app
  integrations](https://help.okta.com/en-us/content/topics/apps/apps_app_integration_wizard_oidc.htm)
  and choose **Web Application**.

  The issuer is your org URL, e.g. `https://example.okta.com`, or the issuer of
  a [custom authorization
  server](https://developer.okta.com/docs/concepts/auth-servers/) such as
  `https://example.okta.com/oauth2/default`.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Microsoft Entra ID">
  Follow [Register an
  application](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/entra/identity-platform/quickstart-register-app)
  with a **Web** redirect URI, then [add a client
  secret](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/entra/identity-platform/quickstart-register-app#add-credentials).
  Copy the secret's **Value**, not its **Secret ID**.

  The issuer is `https://login.microsoftonline.com/<tenant-id>/v2.0`; see
  [OpenID Connect on the Microsoft identity
  platform](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/entra/identity-platform/v2-protocols-oidc).

  Entra doesn't always emit `email` in the ID token. If sign-in fails with a
  missing email, add it as an [optional
  claim](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/entra/identity-platform/optional-claims).
  Entra secrets expire; see [Rotate the client
  secret](#rotate-the-client-secret).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Google Workspace">
  Follow [OpenID Connect](https://developers.google.com/identity/openid-connect/openid-connect)
  to create a **Web application** OAuth client. Set the consent screen's user
  type to **Internal** to restrict sign-in to your organization.

  The issuer is `https://accounts.google.com`.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Configure Redo

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the SSO settings">
  In the [Redo Dashboard](https://app.getredo.com), go to **Settings** →
  **General** → **Single sign-on** and click **Add identity provider**.
</Step>

<Step title="Enter the connection details">
  <ParamField path="Issuer URL" type="string" required>
    The `issuer` value from your provider's discovery document. Redo fetches
    `<issuer>/.well-known/openid-configuration` and validates the `iss` claim
    of every ID token against it.
  </ParamField>

  <ParamField path="Client ID" type="string" required>
    The client ID from your IdP.
  </ParamField>

  <ParamField path="Client secret" type="string" required>
    The client secret from your IdP. Stored encrypted and not shown again
    after saving; enter a new value to replace it.
  </ParamField>

  <ParamField path="Email domains" type="string[]" required>
    Domains, e.g. `example.com`, whose users are sent to your IdP at sign-in.
    Other users continue to sign in as before.
  </ParamField>
</Step>

<Step title="Test and save">
  Click **Test connection** to run a sign-in against your IdP in a new window.
  Redo reports whether it received a valid ID token and which email address it
  contained. Nothing changes for other users until you click **Save**.
</Step>
</Steps>

### Require SSO

By default, users with a matching email domain can still sign in with a
password or email code. Once SSO is confirmed working, turn on **Require SSO**
to disable those methods for matching domains.

<Warning>
  With **Require SSO** on, a broken IdP configuration locks out your whole
  team. Contact [support@getredo.com](mailto:support@getredo.com) and we'll
  turn the requirement off so you can sign in and fix it.
</Warning>

## User matching

Redo matches the ID token's `email` claim, case-insensitively, to a user on
your team (see [Claims](/docs/identity/oidc#claims)). Users aren't created at
sign-in: provision them with
[SCIM](/docs/identity/scim-provisioning) or add them under
**Settings** → **General** → **Users**. Roles come from **Settings** →
**General** → **Roles** or from SCIM groups, not from ID token claims.

## Sessions and sign-out

- Signing out of Redo ends the Redo session only; it doesn't sign the user
  out of your IdP.
- Deactivating a user in your IdP blocks new sign-ins but doesn't end active
  sessions. Deactivation pushed through SCIM ends sessions immediately; without
  SCIM, also remove the user under **Settings** → **General** → **Users**.

## Rotate the client secret

1. Create a new secret in your IdP, leaving the old one valid
2. In Redo, go to **Settings** → **General** → **Single sign-on**, enter the
   new value in **Client secret**, and save
3. Delete the old secret in your IdP

Once a secret expires, every SSO sign-in fails with `invalid_client` until a
new one is saved in Redo.

## Troubleshooting

Every sign-in error message and its cause is listed in the [OpenID Connect
reference](/docs/identity/oidc#errors). The ones below have fixes that aren't
obvious from the message.

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Couldn’t reach your identity provider">
  Discovery failed. Open `<issuer>/.well-known/openid-configuration` in a
  browser: it must return a document whose `issuer` exactly equals the
  **Issuer URL** you entered, including scheme, path, and trailing slash.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Your IdP reports a redirect URI mismatch">
  The registered redirect URI isn't exactly
  `https://app.getredo.com/login/oidc/callback`. Check the scheme, path, and
  trailing slash.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Your identity provider didn’t return an email address">
  Neither the ID token nor UserInfo contained `email`. Confirm the `email`
  scope is granted and the user has an email address in your IdP. Entra ID
  may need `email` as an optional claim.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="No Redo user matches this email address">
  The asserted email isn't on your team. Provision the user with SCIM or add
  them under **Settings** → **General** → **Users** with the exact address
  your IdP sends; invited addresses often differ from the IdP's primary email.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
