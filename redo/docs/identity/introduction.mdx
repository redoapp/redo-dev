## Identity and access

Redo integrates with your identity provider so it can own authentication and
the user lifecycle for the Merchant App:

- **Single sign-on** with OpenID Connect, so your team signs in with your
  IdP instead of a Redo password
- **User provisioning** with SCIM 2.0, so your IdP creates, updates, and
  deactivates users and assigns their roles

Roles and permissions themselves remain in Redo under **Settings** →
**General** → **Roles**.
