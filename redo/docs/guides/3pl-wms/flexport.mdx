## What is Flexport?

Flexport is a global freight forwarder and logistics platform that provides end-to-end supply chain services, including warehousing, fulfillment, and freight forwarding for e-commerce businesses. Built on modern technology, Flexport offers visibility and control across the entire supply chain from factory to customer.

**Primary Purpose:** Flexport delivers comprehensive logistics and fulfillment services for e-commerce brands, combining freight forwarding, warehousing, and last-mile delivery. The platform provides real-time inventory visibility, order fulfillment, and warehouse management capabilities for businesses that need integrated supply chain solutions.

## What Does the Integration Do?

When a customer initiates a return, Redo automatically creates an Advanced Shipping Notice (ASN) or Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) in Flexport, notifying the warehouse that items are being returned. This ensures your warehouse team knows exactly what's coming back and can prepare to receive and process the items efficiently.

Products are mapped using SKUs, returns are linked to original orders, and tracking information is sent to the warehouse to help staff match returns to the correct shipment. Return reasons and processing instructions are included to help warehouse staff process returns correctly—whether items should be restocked, inspected for damage, or handled differently. The integration supports multiple warehouses and multiple shipments per return, giving you flexibility as your business scales across different Flexport fulfillment centers.

## How to Set It Up

Follow these steps to configure the Flexport integration with Redo.

### Prerequisites

Before you begin, ensure you have:

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Active Flexport Account" icon="circle-check">
You must have an active Flexport account with fulfillment services enabled.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Flexport API Credentials" icon="key">
Obtain your Flexport API key and credentials. Contact your Flexport account manager or access your API credentials through the Flexport platform settings.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Products and Orders Synced to Flexport" icon="box">
Your products and orders must already be synced to Flexport, typically through your order management system (OMS), Shopify, or other e-commerce platform. Returns cannot be created for products or orders that don't exist in Flexport's system.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Warehouse Information" icon="warehouse">
Know which Flexport fulfillment center(s) handle your inventory and returns. Returns are automatically routed based on the warehouse that fulfilled the original order.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Product Data Alignment" icon="database">
Ensure your product SKUs in Redo match the SKUs configured in Flexport's system. Products must exist in Flexport's inventory master before returns can be synced.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Configuration Steps

<Steps>
<Step title="Navigate to Integrations">
1. Log in to your Redo merchant dashboard
2. Go to **Settings** > **Returns & Claims** > **Integrations**
3. Locate the **"Flexport"** card in the list of available integrations
</Step>

<Step title="Initiate Connection">
Click the **Connect** button on the Flexport integration card.
</Step>

<Step title="Enter Required Information">
In the configuration form, provide the following information:

<ParamField path="apiKey" type="string" required>
Your Flexport API key for authentication. Keep this credential secure and never share it publicly.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="organizationId" type="string" required>
Your Flexport organization identifier. This can be found in your Flexport account settings or provided by your account manager.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="warehouseId" type="string" required>
The Flexport fulfillment center identifier where returns should be routed. If you use multiple fulfillment centers, you can configure this per shipping location.

**Example:** `FFC-US-LA-01`
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="autoProcessReturns" type="boolean" default="true">
When enabled, Redo will automatically sync the status of ASNs/RMAs from Flexport and either flag or process your returns based on the receiving status automatically.

If disabled, you will need to manually process returns based on Flexport receiving status.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="notifyWarehouse" type="boolean" default="true">
When enabled, Flexport will send notifications to warehouse staff when new return ASNs are created, ensuring visibility into incoming returns.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="testMode" type="boolean" default="false">
Enable for testing without affecting production warehouse operations. When enabled, ASNs are created with a test flag and won't trigger actual warehouse workflows.
</ParamField>
</Step>

<Step title="Configure Shipping Locations">
Link your shipping locations to Flexport warehouses:

1. Go to **Settings** > **Shipping Locations**
2. Find the shipping location that corresponds to your Flexport fulfillment center
3. Click to edit the location
4. In the **Flexport Warehouse ID** field, enter your Flexport fulfillment center identifier
5. Save the changes

This ensures returns are routed to the correct Flexport fulfillment center based on where the original order was shipped from.
</Step>

<Step title="Save Configuration">
Review all settings carefully and click **Save** or **Connect**.
</Step>

<Step title="Test the Integration">
Create a test return to verify the integration is working correctly:

1. Initiate a test return through your Redo return portal
2. Generate a return shipping label
3. Check the return timeline in the Redo dashboard for a Flexport ASN creation entry
4. Log in to Flexport to verify the ASN/RMA was created with correct order linkage, products, quantities, and tracking information
5. Confirm the warehouse received notification of the incoming return
</Step>
</Steps>

## How Long Does It Take?

### Setup Timeline

<CardGroup cols={2}>
<Card title="Initial Setup" icon="clock">
**10-20 minutes**

Time to gather API credentials, organization and warehouse identifiers, and configure the integration settings in Redo.
</Card>

<Card title="ASN/RMA Creation" icon="bolt">
**Real-time (1-3 seconds)**

ASNs are created within seconds of return submission when auto-process is enabled.
</Card>

<Card title="First Return Test" icon="vial">
**5-10 minutes**

Time to create a test return, generate a label, and verify the ASN appears in Flexport with correct details.
</Card>

<Card title="Warehouse Receiving" icon="warehouse">
**Varies by warehouse SLA**

Depends on your Flexport fulfillment center operations and service level agreement. Items are typically scanned and marked received within 1-3 business days of physical arrival.
</Card>
</CardGroup>

### Sync Timing Details

<Steps>
<Step title="ASN/RMA Creation">
ASNs are created **immediately** when a return is processed and a shipping label is generated.

If auto-process is disabled, ASNs are created when returns are manually processed by your team.
</Step>

<Step title="Timeline Entries">
Timeline entries appear in the Redo dashboard **immediately** when ASNs are created, providing instant visibility into the integration activity.
</Step>

<Step title="Receiving Status Updates">
Receiving status updates are polled from Flexport on a scheduled basis and automatically update return status in Redo when items are scanned and received at the fulfillment center.
</Step>

<Step title="Inventory Reconciliation">
Once Flexport marks items as received, the status syncs to Redo, allowing you to close out returns, trigger refunds or exchanges, and reconcile inventory across both platforms.
</Step>
</Steps>
