## What Are Return Integrations?

Return integrations connect your warehouse management system (WMS), third-party
logistics provider (3PL), or fulfillment platform directly to Redo. When a
customer requests a return through Redo, we automatically create a corresponding
return merchandise authorization (RMA) in your external system—no manual data
entry, no duplicate work, and no risk of miscommunication between platforms.

## Why Use Return Integrations?

Integrating your WMS or 3PL with Redo ensures your warehouse team has the
information they need to process returns efficiently:

**Eliminate Manual Data Entry** When a return is approved in Redo, the RMA
details automatically flow to your warehouse system. Your warehouse team sees
what's coming back, when to expect it, and how to process it—without anyone
manually creating tickets or sending spreadsheets.

**Reduce Processing Errors** Automatic synchronization means SKUs, quantities,
and processing instructions are always accurate. No more returns arriving at the
warehouse without proper documentation.

**Maintain Complete Visibility** Track returns across both platforms. When your
warehouse updates return status, those updates can flow back to Redo, giving
everyone—from customer service to operations—a single source of truth.

## What Integrations Do

When you connect your WMS or 3PL to Redo, here's what happens automatically:

**Automatic RMA Creation** When a return is approved in Redo, an RMA is
automatically created in your warehouse system with all relevant
details—customer information, items being returned, return reason, and
processing instructions.

**Automatic Refund and Exchange Processing** Once your warehouse receives and
processes the return in your WMS, Redo automatically triggers the refund or
sends out the exchange item to the customer. No manual intervention needed—the
entire flow from RMA creation to customer refund or exchange fulfillment happens
seamlessly between systems.

## Setup Process

Most integrations follow a similar setup pattern:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Gather Credentials">
    Obtain API credentials from your WMS or 3PL provider. This typically
    includes an API key, endpoint URL, and sometimes a client ID or secret.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure in Redo">
    Navigate to **Settings** → **Integrations** in your Redo dashboard and
    select your provider from the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Connection Details">
    Provide your API credentials and configure sync settings like which
    warehouse location to use or what data fields to include.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How quickly do RMAs appear in my warehouse system?">
    RMAs are typically created within seconds of return approval in Redo. Most
    integrations work in real-time or near-real-time, ensuring your warehouse
    team has immediate visibility into incoming returns.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is my data secure during integration?">
    Yes. All data transfers use encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Redo uses
    secure API authentication provided by your WMS or 3PL. We're SOC 2 Type II
    certified and follow industry best practices for data security.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if my WMS or 3PL isn't listed?">
    If we don't have a pre-built integration for your system, we can often build
    custom integrations for enterprise customers. Contact
    [support@getredo.com](mailto:support@getredo.com) to discuss your needs.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
